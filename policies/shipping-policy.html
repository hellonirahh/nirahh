Where We Ship

We currently ship across India only. International shipping is not available as a standard service but may be arranged on request. International shipping charges will be charged at actuals.

Processing & Delivery

Orders are usually dispatched within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Estimated delivery is 2–4 business days for metro cities and 4–7 business days for other locations. Delivery times may vary due to circumstances beyond our control.

Shipping Charges

Free shipping on orders above ₹5,000 within India.

Orders below ₹5,000 are charged a flat ₹99 shipping fee.

Tracking

Tracking details will be shared by email or SMS once your order is dispatched.

Damaged, Lost or Delayed Orders

If your order arrives damaged, is significantly delayed, or appears to be lost, please contact us at hello.nirahh@gmail.com or +91 97409 16004. For damaged products, please share photographs of the package and product within 24 hours of delivery.

Delivery Address

Please check your address carefully before placing your order. Once dispatched, we may not be able to change the delivery address. Additional shipping charges may apply if re-shipping is required due to an incorrect or incomplete address.
